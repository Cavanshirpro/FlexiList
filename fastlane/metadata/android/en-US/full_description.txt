FlexiList is an ultra-fast, offline-first, modern, modular, and flexible multi-purpose list management and workout assistant application built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

Features:
- Advanced Workout Assistant with DSL parsing for exercises, sets, reps, timers, and target muscle groups.
- Interactive Muscle Group Visualizer with cyberpunk anatomical body graphics.
- Multi-purpose notebook support: To-Do lists, Shopping lists, Habit trackers, and Workout planners.
- SSL-encrypted Cloud Sync and offline JSON backup/restore.
- Multi-language support (English, Turkish, Russian, German, Spanish, French).
- Material Design 3 customizable dark and light themes with custom accent colors.
- Zero proprietary trackers, analytics, or proprietary ad SDKs.
