FlexiList is an ultra-fast, offline-first, modern, and flexible multi-purpose list management and workout assistant application built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and SQLite-based Room Database.

Key Features:

1. Advanced Workout Assistant (Workout Planner):
- Smart DSL Parsing for exercise descriptions (sets, reps/timers, weight, rest duration, target muscle groups).
- Superset support with visual connection badges.
- Distraction-Free Full-Screen Player with preparation countdown, automatic rest timer, and anatomical Muscle Group Visualizer.
- Daily & Monthly program routines.
- Detailed workout session reports and tonnage analytics.

2. Multi-Purpose Notebook & List Management:
- Standard, To-Do, Shopping, Habit Tracker, and Workout Planner notebooks.
- Drag & drop reordering, priority level customization, and bulk selection mode.
- Tag and category filtering.

3. Privacy & Open Source:
- 100% offline-first local Room database storage.
- Zero analytics, trackers, or proprietary ad SDKs. F-Droid compliant.
- SSL-encrypted zero-config cloud sync with unique sync keys.
- Multi-language support (English, Turkish, Russian, German, Spanish, French).
